I'm a pianist in Bern. This winter I'm looking for a regular evening in a hotel bar, a restaurant or a lounge — in Valais, Vaud or around Bern. One fixed night a week suits me best; I'll travel further if it's for the whole season.

I play alone at the piano, or with Quincy Freysinger — voice and guitar — as the duo Laguna Breeze. We've played together for a few years; the duo is what you want when the room is louder, the solo when people are eating. The photo above is a December evening in a chalet lounge: that's roughly the idea.

The practical side

When
From 1 December 2026, for the winter season. One fixed evening a week is what I'd like most; single evenings and private events work too.
Where
Valais, Vaud and Bern first. For a residency of a season I'll travel anywhere in Switzerland.
Sets
Two or three sets of 45 minutes, from aperitif into dinner. Volume to suit the room — people can talk over it, or listen.
Piano
Yours, grand or upright. If there isn't one, I bring a stage piano and a small amp.
Music
Jazz standards, bossa nova, soul, a few of my own pieces. Requests welcome, including the ones you're tired of.
Duo
With Quincy on voice and guitar it gets warmer and a bit louder. Good for bars, après-ski and parties.
Fee
It depends on the place and how many evenings. Ask, and I'll give you a figure the same day — a regular night costs less per evening than a one-off.
