The practical side

When
From 1 December 2026, for the winter season. One fixed evening a week is what I'd like most; single evenings and private events work too.
Where
Valais, Vaud and Bern first. Further for a residency.
Sets
Two or three sets of 45 minutes, from aperitif into dinner. Volume to suit the room — people can talk over it, or listen.
Piano
Yours, grand or upright. If there isn't one, I bring a stage piano and a small amp.
Music
Jazz standards, bossa nova, soul, a few of my own pieces. Requests welcome, including the ones you're tired of.
Duo
With Quincy on voice and guitar it gets warmer and a bit louder. Good for bars, après-ski and parties.
Fee
Depends on the place and the number of evenings. Ask and I'll give you a figure the same day.
